Assistant Buyer – Fresh Produce
Harbour & Hill Foodservice · Salford Quays · Full-time, hybrid (3 days in the office)

(Made-up ad, written as an example for Job Compass. The company does not exist.)

Harbour & Hill supplies fresh fruit, vegetables and dairy to more than 600 restaurants, cafés and schools across the North West.

What you'll do
• Support the Buyer in managing a range of around 400 fresh produce lines
• Place daily orders with growers and wholesalers, based on sales forecasts
• Track supplier performance (on-time delivery, quality, price) and flag issues
• Analyse price movements and support cost negotiations
• Maintain accurate product data in our ordering system
• Work closely with our warehouse, sales and quality teams

What we're looking for
• A strong understanding of fresh produce and seasonality
• Strong numeracy and good Excel skills (VLOOKUP and pivot tables are a plus)
• Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment
• Previous buying or procurement experience is an advantage but not required
• A full UK driving licence would be nice to have, for occasional supplier visits

Salary £27,000–£30,000 depending on experience. Free lunch and discount on our range.
