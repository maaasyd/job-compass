Production Planner
Brightwater Bakery · Oldham · Permanent · Monday to Friday, 8am–4:30pm

(Made-up ad, written as an example for Job Compass. The company does not exist.)

Brightwater Bakery produces 1.2 million bread rolls, cakes and savoury bakes every week for supermarkets and coffee chains.

Your role
Reporting to the Operations Manager, you will create daily and weekly production plans across our three bakery lines, balancing customer orders, labour, raw material availability and line capacity.

You will:
• Build the production schedule in our ERP system each day
• Adjust plans at short notice when orders or ingredients change
• Work with the shift managers to agree staffing levels
• Monitor stock levels of raw materials and packaging
• Report on plan adherence and waste

You will need:
• Experience of planning or scheduling in a food or fast-moving environment
• Excellent Excel skills
• A calm approach under pressure
• Knowledge of SAP or a similar ERP system would be an advantage

Salary: £29,000–£32,000. Free bakery products, 25 days holiday, pension.
