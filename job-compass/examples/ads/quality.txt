About us
Example Foods makes fresh ready meals and sauces for supermarkets across the UK. Our site in Trafford Park employs 240 people.

The role
As Quality Assistant you will support the Technical team to ensure that every product leaving our site is safe, legal and of the right quality. You will carry out daily hygiene audits, check HACCP records on the production lines, take samples for the lab and help investigate customer complaints.

Key responsibilities
• Carry out hygiene and foreign body audits on the production floor
• Verify CCP checks and HACCP documentation
• Take product samples and record results
• Support with root cause analysis for customer complaints
• Help prepare for BRCGS and customer audits
• Brief production colleagues on quality standards

About you
• Level 3 Food Safety qualification is essential
• Experience in a food environment (manufacturing, catering or retail) is essential
• HACCP Level 3 is desirable, or a willingness to complete it
• Good attention to detail and confident using Excel
• Excellent communication skills

What we offer
£26,500 per year, 25 days holiday plus bank holidays, free hot meal every shift, company pension.
