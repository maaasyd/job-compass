How it works

Four short steps, a little less help each time.

It starts with a short chat and ends with your search words and one small next step.

Examples made up for a chef who wants daytime hours.

Get it

Free and open source.

For yourself

  1. Download job-compass.zip from the latest release.
  2. In Claude, open Customize > Skills and upload it.
  3. Say what's going on, for example "I have no idea what job I want."
Download the skill

For someone else

A partner, parent or coach can set it up. The person gets short pages on their phone and doesn't need Claude.

You need a Claude plan with skills and code execution. The ad steps work best in the Claude desktop app. In Claude Code, copy the job-compass folder into ~/.claude/skills/.

Why I built this

Most job tools start once you already know what you want. Too many people get stuck before that.
